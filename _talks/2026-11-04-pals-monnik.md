A public talk for the University of Louisville Department of Physics and Astronomy's Public Astronomy Lecture Series. It covers how spectroscopy turns a galaxy's light into measurements of star formation, how tracers such as H&alpha; emission and ultraviolet light show whether a galaxy has recently changed its pace, and why dust and measurement uncertainties complicate the picture.
